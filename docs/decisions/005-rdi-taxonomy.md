# 005 — The government's 44 sub-sectors, not a list of our own

**Decided** 2026-09-11 · **Status** accepted

## What we decided

Every company is placed in the RDI scheme's own taxonomy — 5 sunrise sectors, 44
sub-sectors and their indicative project types, copied into `rdi-taxonomy.json` from
the DST/ANRF document with light normalisation and nothing added. A company that fits
none of the 44 is not stretched into the nearest one. It goes off the map, filed under
the name of what the classifier said was missing.

## What else we considered

**Our own list, drawn from the companies.** It would fit better. "Computer vision",
"drones", "water", "EV charging" would each get a cell, and far more of the list would
place. It would also be a map of our sources drawn by the people who chose them, and a
coverage map whose cells we invented cannot show a blind spot — every empty cell would
be one we decided to draw.

**The RDI taxonomy plus a few cells of our own.** Rejected for the same reason in a
smaller dose. The moment one invented cell sits beside 44 official ones, a reader can
no longer tell which empty squares are the government's priorities and which are ours.

## Why

The scheme is the vocabulary money is being allocated in. An empty RDI cell is a
national priority where public sources show nothing, which is a finding a fund can use.
An empty cell in our own list would only be a fact about our list.

## What we gave up

Most of the pipeline. On 14 September 2026, 1,678 companies had reached it and 761 were
on the map; 749 were unmapped under the taxonomy and classifier, the largest groups
being computer vision applications (82), IoT platforms (61), enterprise AI (51) and
renewable energy infrastructure (50). Those companies are listed, grouped and counted
on the page, but none of them is in a cell, and a reader looking for drone companies
has to find them off the map.

It also makes the fuller cells partly a map of where two government vocabularies
happen to agree (DPIIT's "Robotics" against the scheme's "Intelligent Systems &
Robotics"), which the page says in its methodology.

## What would change our mind

The scheme revising its taxonomy — the file is replaced, not edited. Or a reader
showing that an unmapped group is really a mis-read of an existing cell, in which case
the fix is the classifier, not a new cell.
